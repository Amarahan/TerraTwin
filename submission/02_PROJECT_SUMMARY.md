## Project Summary (130–160 words — suitable for submission form)

TerraTwin — Planetary Analog Atlas is an open, evidence-based web application that identifies Earth environments whose physical characteristics most closely resemble selected regions on the Moon and Mars. It addresses a core challenge in planetary mission planning: finding accessible terrestrial sites where hardware, suits, and crew procedures can be tested under conditions analogous to target destinations.

The application compares 12 Earth analog candidate sites against four planetary targets — Shackleton Crater Rim, Malapert Massif, Jezero Crater, and Meridiani Planum — across eight scientific features derived from NASA, ESA, JAXA, and CSA datasets: slope, surface roughness, temperature contrast, dryness, vegetation/bareness, rock/mineral type, water ice/permafrost, and dust/aerosol loading.

An exploratory similarity scoring model generates 48 site-target comparisons. All results are explicitly labeled provisional and indicative, pending coordinate confirmation and independent scientific validation. The application prioritizes transparency: null values are preserved, every score is sourced, and known limitations are prominently disclosed.
