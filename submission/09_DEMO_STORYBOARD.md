# 09 — Demo Video Storyboard

**Project:** TerraTwin — Planetary Analog Atlas  
**Target duration:** 30 seconds  
**Live site:** https://amarahan.github.io/TerraTwin/

> **Note:** This is a storyboard and narration script only. An actual MP4 video requires a human team member to perform a screen recording using the live site, following the scene descriptions below. No AI tool can produce a finished screen-capture video of the live application.

---

## Production Notes

**Recording setup:**
- Browser: Chrome or Edge, full-screen, 1440×900 or 1920×1080
- Site: https://amarahan.github.io/TerraTwin/
- Audio: Clear narration or voice-over recorded separately and mixed in
- Suggested tool: OBS Studio (free) or any screen recording software
- Output: MP4, H.264, minimum 720p

**Before recording:**
1. Open the live site and let it fully load
2. Pre-select the target you will demo (e.g., Shackleton Crater Rim)
3. Pre-select a featured site for the Details demo (e.g., Dry Valleys)
4. Clear any browser UI chrome if possible (full-screen mode)

---

## Scene-by-Scene Storyboard

### Scene 1 — Title / Introduction (0:00 – 0:05)

**Screen action:** Site loads. Home / Targets tab is visible. TerraTwin header and hero are shown.

**Narration:**  
*"TerraTwin — an open planetary analog atlas that uses NASA data to find Earth environments that mirror the Moon and Mars."*

**Key visual:** TerraTwin header, navy/cyan design, planetary target cards beginning to appear.

---

### Scene 2 — Planetary Targets Tab (0:05 – 0:10)

**Screen action:** Click to / scroll through the four target cards: Shackleton Crater Rim, Malapert Massif, Jezero Crater, Meridiani Planum. Click one target card to select it.

**Narration:**  
*"We compare 12 Earth analog sites against four planetary targets — two on the Moon, two on Mars."*

**Key visual:** Four target cards with NASA browse imagery labeled "Browse image — contextual only."

---

### Scene 3 — Explore Map (0:10 – 0:15)

**Screen action:** Click Explore tab. The world map appears with 12 provisional dashed markers. Click "Fit All Sites." Hover over a marker to show the tooltip. Click a Myanmar site marker.

**Narration:**  
*"The interactive map shows 12 Earth candidate sites. Coordinates are provisional, pending team confirmation."*

**Key visual:** OSM basemap, 12 dashed markers, tooltip "PENDING_HUMAN_REVIEW," filter buttons.

---

### Scene 4 — Site Details & Scoring (0:15 – 0:22)

**Screen action:** With a site selected, navigate to Details tab. Show the ModelScore badge (with "Provisional" label). Scroll through the 8-feature comparison meters. Show one or two feature readings with source citations.

**Narration:**  
*"Each site is scored against the selected target using 8 NASA-derived features: slope, temperature, dryness, vegetation, rock type, and more. All scores are explicitly labeled exploratory and provisional."*

**Key visual:** ModelScore badge, similarity meter bars for each feature, "Subject to Peer Review" label visible.

---

### Scene 5 — Rankings / Catalogue (0:22 – 0:27)

**Screen action:** Click Rankings tab. The 12-site table appears with conic-gradient circular score badges. Click a group filter (e.g., Featured). Click Inspect on one site.

**Narration:**  
*"The rankings catalogue lets you compare all 12 sites at a glance — filtered by group, with transparent scoring for every site."*

**Key visual:** Rankings table, circular score badges, "Inspect Site →" actions.

---

### Scene 6 — Outro / Live URL (0:27 – 0:30)

**Screen action:** Fade to or stay on a clean screen showing the site URL. Optionally show the Method tab for 1–2 seconds to highlight transparency.

**Narration:**  
*"TerraTwin is live at amarahan.github.io/TerraTwin — open-source, transparent, and built on NASA data."*

**Key visual:** URL displayed clearly. GitHub repo URL optionally shown.
