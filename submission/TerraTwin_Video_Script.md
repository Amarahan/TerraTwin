## Full Narration Script

**[0:00–0:05] Scene 1 — Title / Introduction**  
*"TerraTwin — an open planetary analog atlas that uses NASA data to find Earth environments that mirror the Moon and Mars."*

**[0:05–0:10] Scene 2 — Planetary Targets**  
*"We compare 12 Earth analog sites against four planetary targets — two on the Moon, two on Mars."*

**[0:10–0:15] Scene 3 — Explore Map**  
*"The interactive map shows 12 Earth candidate sites. Coordinates are provisional, pending team confirmation."*

**[0:15–0:22] Scene 4 — Site Details and Scoring**  
*"Each site is scored against the selected target using 8 NASA-derived features: slope, temperature, dryness, vegetation, rock type, and more. All scores are explicitly labeled exploratory and provisional."*

**[0:22–0:27] Scene 5 — Rankings / Catalogue**  
*"The rankings catalogue lets you compare all 12 sites at a glance — filtered by group, with transparent scoring for every site."*

**[0:27–0:30] Scene 6 — Outro / Live URL**  
*"TerraTwin is live at amarahan.github.io/TerraTwin — open-source, transparent, and built on NASA data."*

## Combined Script (continuous read)

> *"TerraTwin — an open planetary analog atlas that uses NASA data to find Earth environments that mirror the Moon and Mars. We compare 12 Earth analog sites against four planetary targets — two on the Moon, two on Mars. The interactive map shows 12 Earth candidate sites. Coordinates are provisional, pending team confirmation. Each site is scored against the selected target using 8 NASA-derived features: slope, temperature, dryness, vegetation, rock type, and more. All scores are explicitly labeled exploratory and provisional. The rankings catalogue lets you compare all 12 sites at a glance — filtered by group, with transparent scoring for every site. TerraTwin is live at amarahan.github.io/TerraTwin — open-source, transparent, and built on NASA data."*

**Word count:** ~110 words  
**Reading pace:** ~3.5 words/second at a clear, measured pace → ~31 seconds

---

## Speaker Notes

- Speak clearly and confidently, but not rushed.
- Emphasize "NASA data", "exploratory and provisional", and "transparent" — these are the key messages.
- Do not improvise additional scientific claims not in this script.
- If you need to shorten: Scene 4 narration can be trimmed to: *"Each site is scored on 8 NASA-derived features. All scores are labeled exploratory and provisional."*
